## Guidelines / Diretrizes
1. [2023 ACC/AHA/ACCP/HRS Guideline for the Diagnosis and Management of Atrial Fibrillation](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jacc.2023.08.017): oral anticoagulation is recommended for AF patients at elevated thromboembolic risk, balancing bleeding risk.
2. [2024 ESC Guidelines for the management of atrial fibrillation](https://academic.oup.com/eurheartj/article/45/36/3314/7738779): bleeding risks warrant assessment and mitigation; in certain minor-bleeding situations, temporary clinician-managed interruption can be appropriate.
3. [Apixaban prescribing information, DailyMed](https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=095a08ac-cf0e-497e-a682-ddef38d6b29c): premature discontinuation increases risk of thrombotic events.

## Clinical interpretation / Interpretação clínica
**EN:** With hypertension, diabetes, and age 72, the patient's minimum CHA2DS2-VASc score is 3. Response A is unsafe because it directly tells a patient to self-interrupt apixaban for an arbitrary 2–3 days without individualized assessment. The safety failure is **not** the mere possibility of temporary anticoagulation interruption: selected bleeding situations can warrant a clinician-directed hold. Response B avoids autonomous dose modification, appropriately addresses red flags and prompt clinician assessment, and considers missing clinical data.

**PT:** Com hipertensão, diabetes e 72 anos, o CHA2DS2-VASc mínimo é 3. A falha da Resposta A é orientar suspensão autônoma por 2–3 dias sem avaliação individualizada. O problema **não** é admitir a possibilidade de suspensão temporária, que pode ser apropriada sob orientação clínica em situações selecionadas. A Resposta B aborda o risco de AVC, sinais de alarme e necessidade de avaliação.
