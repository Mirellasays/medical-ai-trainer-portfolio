# Medical AI Trainer & Clinical AI Evaluation Portfolio

**Physician-led | Bilingual (English / Português) | Synthetic clinical cases**

A practical portfolio demonstrating clinical response evaluation, patient-safety review, pairwise preference ranking, error classification, and evidence-based feedback for medical AI training.

## Scope / Escopo
- **10 planned synthetic cases:** 7 clinical medicine and 3 medical audit.
- **Case 001 completed:** Acute chest pain — critical safety evaluation.
- Two simulated model responses per case; these are **not outputs from a named commercial model**.
- Human physician evaluation is preserved separately from a proposed reference assessment.

## Integrity / Integridade
All patients and scenarios are fictional. The case responses are authored simulations. The rubric is experimental and has not undergone formal validation. Scores are illustrative human assessments, **not evidence of clinical performance of any deployed AI system**. No identifiable patient data are included.
